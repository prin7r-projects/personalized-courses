# 05 · Audience profile

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

The narrowest possible ICP that still funds the studio:

- **Adult, 28–48.** Has at least one full-time job's worth of context to contend with — career, family, side projects.
- **Self-directed.** Has tried at least two online learning products and abandoned them.
- **Skill-deepening, not credential-chasing.** Wants to ship a thing, not earn a certificate.
- **Comfortable spending $40–$300 / month on themselves** without asking for a manager's approval.
- **At least one of**: lives outside North America (where card processing is expensive), works remotely, has a high-trust private tutor or coach already, or is a senior IC with a self-directed bias.

Why this ICP works: the buyer has already _internalized_ that mass-market learning failed for them. They are not shopping for "the cheapest course"; they are shopping for "a course that works for me, this time." The studio's price ceiling matches that mindset; the price floor is set by the LLM-and-tutor cost.

## Secondary ICPs (we serve them, but not first)

1. **Engineering managers** buying for a 5–15 person team. Volume rate, twelve-month commitment, one-page agreement. (See `/docs/07-sales-strategy.md` for the pricing.)
2. **Private tutors with a small client list** who want to give their students structured drips between live calls. The studio works as a "syllabus + grading layer" with the tutor as the human-in-the-loop.

## Anti-personas (we will not serve these well; we should turn them away politely)

1. **The credential collector.** Wants a certificate, a logo to put on LinkedIn, a seal with the words "AI / blockchain / quantum" on it. We don't issue certificates. We don't put a stamp on anything. The artifacts you make _are_ the credential.
2. **The "I just want a free trial" learner.** We have a refund clause, not a trial. The pricing is the qualifying signal — we cannot afford to draft a syllabus for someone who isn't sure they'll commit a month.
3. **The "build me an MBA in 30 days" learner.** Goals must be narrow enough that one declared sentence is honest. "Become a CTO in 6 weeks" is not a goal we can build a syllabus around. We will say so kindly.
4. **The platform shopper.** Someone evaluating us against Coursera-for-Business as a vendor RFP. We do not write RFP responses. The "For teams" block in pricing is the entire procurement experience, by design.

## Geographic and language coverage

- **Default supported languages (Wave 2)**: English, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Polish, Indonesian.
- **On request**: French, German, Italian, Korean, Vietnamese, Hindi (within 48-hour confirmation).
- **Geographic strength**: Iberian peninsula, LATAM (especially MX/CO/CL/AR), Eastern Europe, South Asian English-medium, Indonesia.
- **Geographic weakness — by design**: the United States. The studio doesn't compete on US card-payment optimization, mass-marketing budgets, or aggressive "credential" framing. We can serve US buyers — many will buy — but we don't tune the marketing to them.
