02 The problem

02 · The problem

Mass-market online learning has failed adult self-directed learners.

  • Coursera completion rates are below 5%.
  • Buyers have backlogs of unfinished courses they feel guilty about.
  • The mass-market course is built for an imagined average student, not a real one.

The crisis is not access. The crisis is fit.

03 The buyer

03 · The buyer's reality

Mara, 34, backend engineer in Lisbon.

  • Wants to ship a Rust CLI in 30 days.
  • Has bought two Udemy courses, finished none.
  • Read half the Klabnik & Nichols book; got stuck at lifetimes.
  • Has 4–6 hours of study a week, and a deadline.
  • Is willing to pay. Cannot find a course built for her.

The studio is sized to serve Mara.

04 The product

04 · The product

One declared goal in, one personalized syllabus out.

Intake
1 sentence on the goal · 1 paragraph on level · cadence + language
Syllabus
4 modules max, on a single page, locked after one revision round
Drip
Mondays at 09:00 buyer's local time, asynchronous
Each module
1 reading + 1 video + 1 exercise + 1 self-evaluation
End
A graded artifact the buyer keeps · refundable in the first week

$149 single course · $39/mo standing · $299/mo coach-augmented.

05 Why now

05 · Three forces converging

2025–2026 is the first window where this is unit-economic.

  • LLMs got good at structured output. GLM 5.1 Flash + Claude 4.7 reliably draft a syllabus that passes editorial review with light edits.
  • Citation infrastructure got open. Crossref + OpenLibrary cover ~80% of named readings. We can verify before drip.
  • Adult learners got disenchanted. The Coursera certificate trade has failed. Buyers want craft.

07 Distribution

07 · How buyers find us

We earn distribution by publishing useful artefacts.

  • One public syllabus per quarter (CC-BY) — anchors HN / subreddit posts.
  • One paid newsletter placement per quarter (Bytes, TLDR, Programmer Brain).
  • The desk (`tutor@`, `cohorts@`) — closes 60% of replied conversations.
  • Tutor-affiliate program — working tutors bring their clients.

We do not buy keyword search.
